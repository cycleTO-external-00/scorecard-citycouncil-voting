Local democracy and Toronto’s autonomy are under attack, but City Hall cannot stand still. This October, Cycle Toronto is asking candidates to pledge to make it easier and more convenient for more people to choose to ride a bike along our existing bike lanes and trails citywide.

      Cycle Toronto is also committed to working with City Council on:

      • Cycling Network Plan expansion despite the current provincial restrictions under Bill 60
      • Enhancing and connecting multi-use paths and trails, including along The Meadoway, The Loop, West Toronto Rail Path, West Scarborough Rail Trail, and Etobicoke Greenway
      • Aligning city by-laws around e-bikes and other micromobility vehicles pending provincial regulations, and advocating to the federal government on mandatory lithium-ion battery safety standards
      • Holding app-based delivery companies accountable for their riders and general safety, and focusing on education over enforcement
      • Reallocating limited police resources from harassing people on bikes towards working to achieve our Vision Zero objectives
